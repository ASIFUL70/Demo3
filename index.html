Market Research Case Study

Retail Market Feasibility & Consumer Survey Analysis

Business Objective:

Determine market demand and pricing sensitivity for expanding an eco-friendly consumer goods brand into three secondary metropolitan markets.

My Individual Role:

Designed survey questionnaire, sampled 1,250 respondents, cleansed raw datasets using Excel, and performed cross-tabulation and regression analysis in SPSS.

Key Tools & Methods:

SPSS, Tableau, Excel PivotTables, Likert Scale Survey Design, Chi-Square Testing

Main Recommendations:

Recommended prioritizing Location B based on higher price tolerance ($45-$60 band) and a 34% higher purchase intent index among young professionals.

Finance Case Study

Corporate DCF Valuation & Sensitivity Analysis

Business Objective:

Evaluate fair enterprise value for a regional consumer electronics retailer to support buy-side acquisition deliberations in an advanced corporate finance capstone.

My Individual Role:

Calculated weighted average cost of capital (WACC = 8.75%), projected 5-year free cash flows, and constructed dynamic Excel sensitivity matrices for terminal growth rates.

Key Tools & Methods:

Financial Modeling, DCF Valuation, WACC Calculation, Multiples Analysis (EV/EBITDA, P/E)

Main Recommendations:

Determined enterprise valuation of $42.8M ($14.20/share), indicating the target firm was undervalued by 12% relative to current market trading price.
