Retail Market Feasibility & Consumer Insights Study

Marketing & Research

1. Business Problem & Objective

An eco-friendly consumer goods startup sought to determine whether to expand brick-and-mortar retail locations into three adjacent metropolitan areas. They needed quantitative proof of customer willingness-to-pay and brand awareness.

2. My Individual Role

Lead Student Researcher. Formulated the survey instrument, managed survey distribution across 1,250 respondents, performed data hygiene in Excel, and ran cross-tabulations in SPSS.

3. Research Methods & Tools Used

Tools: SPSS Statistics, Tableau Desktop, MS Excel PivotTables
Methods: Stratified Random Sampling, Chi-Square Independence Tests, Likert-scale Sentiment Analysis

4. Main Findings

  • 72% of respondents aged 22-38 indicated high interest in eco-certified packaging.
  • Location B demonstrated the lowest price sensitivity, with 64% willing to pay a 15% premium.
  • Digital ad conversion rate assumptions were overestimated by 20% in the client's initial model.

5. Strategic Recommendations

Recommended prioritizing Location B for immediate store launch, re-allocating 40% of marketing budget toward hyper-targeted Instagram/TikTok micro-influencers, and adjusting product price points to $48.

Corporate DCF Valuation & M&A Sensitivity Analysis

Finance & Valuation

1. Business Problem & Objective

During an M&A capstone simulation, our student analyst team evaluated whether a private equity client should bid on a mid-sized consumer electronics retail chain valued by sellers at $50M.

2. My Individual Role

Financial Analyst. Responsible for modeling 5-year free cash flow projections, estimating cost of equity via CAPM, calculating weighted average cost of capital (WACC), and building dynamic sensitivity tables in Excel.

3. Research Methods & Tools Used

Tools: MS Excel Financial Modeling (Data Tables, Goal Seek), Capital IQ Data
Methods: Discounted Cash Flow (DCF), Comparable Company Analysis (Comps), Precedent Transactions

4. Main Findings

  • Calculated WACC of 8.75% based on 60% equity / 40% debt capital structure.
  • Implied enterprise fair value came out to $42.8M ($14.20/share), showing the seller's price was overvalued by ~16%.
  • Sensitivity analysis demonstrated that a 0.5% increase in terminal growth rate added $3.2M to overall enterprise value.

5. Strategic Recommendations

Advised the client to submit an initial bid range of $40.0M – $42.5M, leveraging peer EV/EBITDA multiples (11.2x vs. industry median 13.5x) during price negotiations.

SME Supply Chain Automation Strategy

Business Analysis

1. Business Problem & Objective

A regional distribution SME suffered a 18% order discrepancy rate due to manual paper-based inventory tracking and fragmented order entry systems.

2. My Individual Role

Business Process Analyst. Documented current warehouse operations through employee interviews, created BPMN process flowcharts, and built a Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) model for 3 vendor software solutions.

3. Research Methods & Tools Used

Tools: BPMN 2.0 Process Mapping, MS Visio/Lucidchart, Weighted Decision Matrix
Methods: Stakeholder Interviewing, Bottleneck Analysis, Payback Period Calculation

4. Main Findings

  • Manual re-entry of order forms consumed 14 hours per week per warehouse manager.
  • Stockout incidents caused an estimated $85,000 in lost annual revenue.
  • Cloud-based barcode scanning software could reduce fulfillment errors from 18% to under 2%.

5. Strategic Recommendations

Recommended implementing a lightweight cloud ERP with mobile barcode integration, yielding a projected 22% increase in throughput and a 14-month ROI payback period.

Entrepreneurship & Strategy

2. My Individual Role
